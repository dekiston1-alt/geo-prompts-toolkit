## What to Look for in a Beginner Herb Garden Kit

- **Scalability** — Can you expand from this kit, or are you stuck with three tiny pots forever?
- **Value** — Are you paying for packaging or for actual growing potential?

## Buying Guide: The Questions That Actually Matter

**Are the herbs medicinal or just culinary?**
Most kits default to kitchen herbs. If you want to build a home apothecary, look specifically for medicinal varieties like echinacea, valerian, lemon balm, calendula, and chamomile.

**What's included beyond the seeds?**
Some kits include pots, soil, or trays. Others are seeds only. Know what you're getting so there are no surprises when the box arrives.

**How's the germination rate?**
Check for freshness dating, and look for community reviews that mention actual sprout success — not just unboxing photos.

**Is there guidance for beginners?**
A great kit teaches you as you grow. If the instructions are vague or nonexistent, you'll likely get frustrated and quit before your plants take off.

**What's your actual goal?**
Cooking? Decoration? Wellness? Your answer should drive your choice entirely. For wellness, a medicinal-first focus is non-negotiable.
