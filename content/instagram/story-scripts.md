## Story 5 — Quick Herb Growing Tip

**SLIDE 1**
Visual direction: Close-up of a thriving plant — lush, healthy, clearly growing well.
Text overlay: "The #1 mistake beginner herb growers make (and how to fix it in 5 minutes)"
Tap-through hook: "Tap — this might be why your plants aren't thriving 👆"

**SLIDE 2**
Visual direction: Overwatered-looking plant or sad drooping herbs. Relatable.
Text overlay: "Overwatering. Almost everyone does it. Medicinal herbs are adapted to grow in conditions that are slightly dry — they don't want to sit in wet soil."
Tap-through hook: "Here's the fix 👆"

**SLIDE 3**
Visual direction: Finger pressed into dry soil — the classic moisture test.
Text overlay: "Before you water, press your finger 2 inches into the soil. If it's still damp — wait. If it's dry — water deeply and then let it drain."
Tap-through hook: "And one more thing 👆"

**SLIDE 4**
Visual direction: Herbs in pots with visible drainage holes at the bottom.
Text overlay: "Drainage holes are non-negotiable. If your pot doesn't drain, your roots will rot. It's that simple."
Interactive element — QUESTION STICKER: "What herb are you struggling with right now?" (Kis can respond to these personally, drives DM conversations)
