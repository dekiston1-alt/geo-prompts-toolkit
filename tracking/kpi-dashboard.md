## WHAT "GOOD" LOOKS LIKE vs. WARNING SIGNS

### Pinterest Impressions

| Status | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| Green (Good) | Growing week over week. Week 2+ showing 3–5x week 1. Boards are indexed. |
| Yellow (Watch) | Flat or slow growth. Impressions not compounding. |
| Red (Warning) | Week 3 impressions lower than week 1. Pins not showing up in search. |

**Quick Action if Red:** Audit your pin titles and descriptions — are you using keyword-rich text? Check that boards are not set to secret. Post at higher frequency. Try fresh image styles.

---

### Pinterest Save Rate

| Status | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| Green (Good) | 1%+ save rate. Specific pins consistently getting saves. |
| Yellow (Watch) | Under 0.5%. Gets impressions but nothing is resonating. |
| Red (Warning) | Under 0.2% across all pins after 2 weeks. |

**Quick Action if Red:** Your visuals or titles aren't connecting. Study the top 3 pins in your niche. Mirror their format (not their content). Test text-heavy vs. image-only pins. Try a different color palette.

---

### Instagram Follower Growth

| Status | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| Green (Good) | +50 or more new followers per week by week 2. Reels getting 500+ views. |
| Yellow (Watch) | +10–25/week. Reach is limited. Content not spreading beyond existing followers. |
| Red (Warning) | Flat or losing followers. Reach consistently under 200. |

**Quick Action if Red:** Post more Reels (they get organic reach, static posts do not). Use trending audio. Check if your hook in the first 3 seconds is strong enough — most people drop off before second 4. Study your top-performing post and make 3 variations of it.

---

### IG Profile Visits → Bio Link Clicks

| Status | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| Green (Good) | 5–10% of profile visits clicking bio link |
| Yellow (Watch) | Under 3% clicking through |
| Red (Warning) | Lots of reach, almost no profile visits |

**Quick Action if Red:** Your content isn't converting curiosity to profile visits. Add a CTA at the end of every Reel: "Link in bio for [specific thing]." Make your bio crystal clear — what you offer, who you help, what to click.

---

### Email Open Rate

| Status | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| Green (Good) | 35%+ open rate. Consistent week over week. |
| Yellow (Watch) | 25–35%. Decent but room to improve. |
| Red (Warning) | Under 20%. Subscribers not engaging. |

**Quick Action if Red:** Test subject lines — make them more personal ("Kis here — I wanted to share something") or more curiosity-driven. Send at different times (try 6–7am Tuesday or Thursday). Check that emails aren't going to spam. Re-engage cold subs with a "still interested?" email.

---

### Affiliate Conversion Rate

| Status | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| Green (Good) | 2%+ conversion. 1 in 50 clicks becomes a sale. |
| Yellow (Watch) | 0.5–2%. Getting clicks, some sales but inconsistent. |
| Red (Warning) | Under 0.5% after 100+ clicks. Traffic but no conversions. |

**Quick Action if Red:** The audience needs more warming before clicking. Add more trust-building content before the CTA. Publish a genuine personal review post or video. Add social proof (share what others say). Check the landing page experience — is there a disconnect between what you're promising and what they find?

## QUICK REFERENCE: WHAT TO DO IF UNDERPERFORMING

| Metric Underperforming | Immediate Action |
|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Pinterest impressions flat | Add 5 keyword-rich words to every pin title. Re-examine board names. |
| Pinterest saves low | Change pin design. Try text-heavy overlays vs. photo-only. |
| IG reach low | Post a Reel (not a static post). Use trending audio. Stronger hook. |
| IG followers not growing | Go comment on 10–15 posts in niche daily for 7 days. Collaborations. |
| Email subscribers not growing | Add a lead magnet. Try a freebie (herb guide PDF, growing checklist). |
| Email open rate low | Rewrite subject lines. More personal, more curiosity. |
| Clicks but no sales | Write a deeper personal review. Add more pre-sell content before link. |
| No clicks at all | Double check bio link is active and correct. Post more CTAs in content. |
