**Focus:** Get everything live and out the door. No more preparing. Today you START.

---

- [ ] **Create 3 Pinterest boards** (20 min) — "Medicinal Herb Garden," "Holistic Wellness for Women," "Natural Remedies at Home." Each with keyword-rich descriptions.
- [ ] **Post first 2 Pinterest pins** (30 min) — Pin 1: "5 Medicinal Herbs Every Woman Should Grow." Pin 2: "What Is the Medicinal Garden Kit? (Honest Review)." Use vertical 2:3 ratio, Canva templates, warm earthy tones.
- [ ] **Film Reel Script #1** (30 min) — The $300/month supplement pain point script. Natural lighting, close crop. Upload and schedule.
- [ ] **Set up email platform** (20 min) — If not done: create ConvertKit/Mailchimp/Beehiiv account. Set up welcome email. Add affiliate link with warm context.

### Afternoon Tasks (1.5 hours)

- [ ] **Publish Instagram Reel** (15 min) — Post Reel Script #1. Write caption in Kis voice. Add bio link reminder. Use Hashtag Set A + C.
- [ ] **Post 3 IG Stories** (20 min) — Story 1: "I have something to share with you" teaser. Story 2: Poll — "Do you spend $50+ on supplements a month?" Story 3: Quick preview of what's coming.
- [ ] **Write and send Welcome Email** (30 min) — Introduce yourself. Tell the $300 story. Share affiliate link naturally. Keep it warm and conversational, like texting a friend.
- [ ] **Join 3 Pinterest group boards** (20 min) — Search for herbal wellness, holistic health, natural living group boards and request to join.

### Evening Tasks (30 min)

- [ ] **Engage** — Reply to every comment and DM. Even if you have zero, check accounts you're inspired by and leave thoughtful comments.
- [ ] **Screenshot your setup** — Document day 1 baseline: follower count, Pinterest impressions (0), email subscribers.

### Key Metric to Hit Today
First Pinterest pin live. Instagram Reel posted. Email welcome sequence active. Bio link confirmed working.

### What "Done" Looks Like
- 2 pins posted on Pinterest
- 1 Reel posted on Instagram
- 3 IG stories up
- Welcome email sent (or scheduled for first subscriber)
- Bio link working and tracked

"Done is better than perfect. Your first pin doesn't have to be your best pin — it has to exist. The algorithm rewards consistency, not perfection. You cannot edit a blank page. Post it."
